About This Book

Femi Alagbada is a 32-year-old VI CEO. His father's will: stay married for one year or lose 70% shares of Alagbada Tech. Amara Eze is a 26-year-old Yaba Bolt driver with a 4.9-star rating and a broken Corolla — her mother is in LUTH, and the hospital bill is ₦2.5 million. They meet on Third Mainland Bridge. Four hours of traffic. No movement.

He offers her ₦10 million to be his contract wife for one year — ₦2.5M upfront, ₦7.5M at the end. She accepts. What starts as a cold business contract in a Yaba lawyer's office turns into Ikoyi dinners, Landmark Beach dates, boardroom wars, and one gala kiss in Computer Village that breaks all the rules.

From fake contract to real love. From Yaba to VI. From an 8k ride to 5 stars forever — a hilarious, emotional, very Nigerian fake-marriage romance about a CEO who learns that the best deals are not made in the boardroom, but in traffic.
